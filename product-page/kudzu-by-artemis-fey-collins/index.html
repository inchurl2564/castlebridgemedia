Kudzu

by Artemis Fey Collins

Releases January 19, 2027

Pre-order on Amazon

A young woman returns to her childhood home to care for her newly divorced, mentally unwell mother, only to find that the paranormal entity who now haunts the home is the least of her worries.

Fawn always assumed that divorce would be good for her parents, but she’s beginning to realize how far-fetched that hope truly was. After receiving a concerning phone call, Fawn rushes back to her childhood home in the secluded Georgia woods to spend time with her family, hoping to encourage her volatile mother to seek mental health help and meet her mother’s new, younger boyfriend. Upon arrival, Fawn quickly discovers that a malevolent energy has taken residence within the shadows of the home, and she must face her childhood fears and reckon with her family’s tragic past in order to keep her mother safe. By including fictional retellings of the author’s own family history, this heart-wrenching novel explores the unique intersection of dysfunctional family dynamics and surreal, atmospheric horror.
