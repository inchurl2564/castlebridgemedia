Everyone Is Worried

by Sandra J. Paul

Buy on Amazon



When life turns its back on you, darkness lurks in every corner

A novel about loss and despair and how far some will go to deal with that loss.

After suffering a great loss, a young woman loses everything that matters to her: her business, her husband and her sanity. Bereft and lost, she turns to an unlikely new ally, a woman who came into her life when she needed someone the most. But is Vivian who she claims to be, or does she have a hand in controlling Zoey’s new life?
