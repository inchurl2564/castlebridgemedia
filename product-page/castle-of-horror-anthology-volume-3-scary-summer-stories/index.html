THE CASTLE IS BACK—AND SUMMERING AT THE BEACH!

It's summertime—and the Castle of Horror Anthology is BACK with a collection of horror stories with a summer theme. If you're headed to the beach, watch out for our collection of ghostly gothic tales, deadly toys, time warps, wasp women and other sun-drenched terrors.

Featuring stories from David Bowles, Jason Henderson, P.J. Hoover, Leanna Renee Hieber, Sam Knight, Alethea Kontis, Jo Whittemore, Bonnie Jo Shufflebeam, John Peel, Drew Wolle and In Churl Yo.
