Hall-Lore-Ween: Thrice The Fright

by Josh Spero

A Castling Books title for young readers

Young Readers

Buy on AmazonRead a sample

Jacko returns with more of his midnight mischief… bearing newly unearthed tales from the ancient scrolls of Hall-Lore-Ween to guide you deeper into the heart of Halloween.

In this third installment of the Hall-Lore-Ween series, the magic of the season burns brighter, while the mysteries run deeper. With a brand-new collection of haunting tales, spellbinding poems, and more spooky illustrations than ever before, this book invites you beyond the friendly, familiar glow of pumpkin light… and into a darker labyrinth of frights and delights.

Inside, you’ll step into stories where quiet nights don’t stay quiet for long… where familiar places begin to feel wrong… and where something as simple as a small town or a knock at the door can unravel into something far more sinister. From eerie encounters with things that shouldn’t exist to unsettling discoveries buried deep within families and forgotten pasts, each tale pulls you further into the unknown, brought vividly to life through haunting illustrations along the way.

Whether you’ve followed Jacko from the very beginning or are just now finding your way to his flame, every tale stands on its own, ready to draw you in, one flicker at a time. And for longtime readers… a few secrets may be waiting just beneath the surface.

Open the book… and let the season take hold.
The next chapter of Hall-Lore-Ween begins now.
