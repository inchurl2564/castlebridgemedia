Play Role Play

by In Churl Yo

Literary Fiction

Buy on Amazon

A Sharp, Funny, and Deeply Felt Story about Grief, Gaming, and Family

Hattie’s mom left her a hidden letter, a deadline, and a list of chores that reads like a quest log: three stops through the places that shaped their family, to “recover what was lost.” Hattie has spent two years running from Seattle, and now she has to go back, with a father who’s always been better at writing about love than living it along for the ride.

With her oldest friends in tow, Hattie works her way through a tour of family memories, and her long dormant love of tabletop role playing comes roaring back. Soon the campaigns she thought she’d outgrown are spilling into the real world, and it gets harder and harder to tell where the quest ends and the memory begins.

For fans of the family chaos of Where’d You Go, Bernadette and the big heart of A Man Called Ove, Play Role Play is part nostalgic adventure, part family comedy, and part love letter to the games that raised us. It treats grief and depression honestly and includes an author’s note with mental health resources.
