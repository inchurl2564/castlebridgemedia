The Kings of Yellow Street

by P.F. White

Buy on Amazon

The King family is not your average family. John, the family patriarch, is a lifelong serial-killer with a jolly disposition and an infectious smile. Cassandra, his wife, is a practicing witch with the attention of several dark, cosmic entities of unfathomable power. Damien, their adopted son, is going to rule the world some day, though he thinks the title of “Anti-Christ” is simply ridiculous. Their adopted daughter, Endrah, is something far worse and far stranger still. Together they are seeking a fresh start in a quiet Californian town. Unfortunately, the horrors of modern school, employment, and suburban living may prove more difficult than anything they have tried before. Thankfully, there is always murder.
