Child of Dark Water

by E.G. Rand

Buy on Amazon

"Child of the Dark Water had me feeling unsettled right from chapter one and I loved it! The idea of the lurking paranormal waiting in the depths is going to stay with me for a long time. E.G Rand nailed it." -- Chadwick Poulsen, founder of Visceral Imagination

“From the murky depths of E.G. Rand’s wicked imagination comes a haunting tale of Lovecraftian horror, witchery, and revenge. Once her Child of the Dark Water gets its slimy black tendrils around your neck, you won’t be able to scream, much less breathe!” -- Christopher Micklos, author, Tick Town

In this supernatural crime thriller, a sleepy town is faced with an ancient evil from the bottom of their lake.

Alice Goodwin became a cop in the lakeside town of Ridgeway to escape the pain of her past. But when a pregnant teenager is killed and thrown into a lake, something horrible is created. Now the dark water seeks revenge, and only Alice can stop the creature from destroying the town.
