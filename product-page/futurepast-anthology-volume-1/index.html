Futurepast Anthology Volume 1

Science FictionAnthologies

Buy on Amazon

Welcome to the Futurepast Anthology! We’ve gathered some of the biggest names in Science Fiction (Asimov! Bradbury! Herbert! Vonnegut!) to celebrate the future visions of yesterday. Futurepast is a collection of some of the greatest science fiction writers writing at their pulpiest best in magazines like Galaxy Science Fiction and Amazing Science Fiction Stories. And while these stories may have been written in the past, all of them are set squarely in a future that’s still as relevant and exhilarating as the day these words were first printed into existence.
