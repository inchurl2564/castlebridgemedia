Hellrise

by Jay Gould

Buy on Amazon

Months after barely escaping the Other Place—a dimension overlapping our own where monstrous entities feed on human souls—Lee Mitchell and his friends have been trying to warn the world that Hell is real. No one believes them. But when a demonic face caught on a New York City smartphone camera matches one Lee saw in Hell itself, the nightmare becomes undeniable.

Hell hasn’t been left behind.

Hell has followed them back.

HELLRISE continues the pulse-pounding saga that began with HELLFALL, combining supernatural horror and military intrigue in a fast-paced story of survival, belief, and cosmic terror. Fans of The Mist, Event Horizon, and Stranger Things will feel right at home—and anything but safe.
