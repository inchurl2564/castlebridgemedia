Hellfall

by Jay Gould

Buy on Amazon

From Noodles author Jay Gould comes Hellfall, a heart-pounding horror adventure that combines the monstrous madness of DOOM with the classic tale of JOURNEY TO THE CENTER OF THE EARTH. When an explosion destroys a drilling rig, an expert drill engineer must join an expedition down the hole the rig has opened up. What they discover is a tunnel to a demon-infested world that clearly influenced ancient art, and may be linked to the very origins of humanity itself. As the team navigates the treacherous depths, they must also confront their own inner demons, making Hellfall a chilling and thrilling read from start to finish. Immerse yourself in a world of terror and suspense with this latest novel from Jay Gould.
