About Castle Bridge Media

Castle Bridge Media connects stories, data and the people who love them.

Publishing

We publish horror, mystery, fantasy and thriller fiction for adults and young readers, working closely with authors to find and grow their audiences.

Podcasts

We produce podcasts about entertainment and the people who create it: conversations for fans who want to go deeper into what they watch, read and play.

Data

We build data-driven websites and tools that help people understand culture and entertainment: what audiences are discovering, rediscovering and talking about, and why. Our sites gather public data, turn it into clear, sourced stories, and keep themselves up to date every day.

What guides us

We're curious about why stories last. We champion our authors, give our guests room to talk, and show our sources. Everything we make is for readers, listeners and fans first.
